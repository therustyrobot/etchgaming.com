Building hardware for the table.

Etch Gaming grew out of a simple frustration: cardboard chits and loose dice kept getting lost, cracked, or borrowed and never returned. So we started cutting our own — and other players kept asking where to get them.

Every order is still cut, sanded, and finished in-house, one sheet at a time. Small-batch by choice — it's the only way to keep quality exactly where we want it.

No prints, no stickers. Just clean edges and engraving deep enough to last as long as the game does.
